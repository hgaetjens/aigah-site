Mentored projects

Projects from the Sentient Futures Project Incubator

September to November 2026

From September to November 2026, AIGAH mentored three people in the Sentient Futures Project Incubator, an eleven-week programme in which each participant works on a project of their own. Each of the three projects has a page of its own, and AIGAH adds new material to the pages as it is published.

AI sandboxes and animal welfare law: a submission to the UK Animal Sentience Committee

Dr Laura Thomas-Walters · Submitted 7 October 2026

Laura Thomas-Walters asked the Animal Sentience Committee to examine the UK government's planned regulatory sandboxes, because the published plans do not name animal welfare among the protections that a sandbox has to leave in place.

Farm automation failures: an evidence bank and a review of reporting duties

Sinta Fragkou · 22 incidents documented

Sinta Fragkou documented 22 incidents in which automated farm systems failed and animals died, and she compared the legal duties to report farmed animal deaths in ten European jurisdictions.

Automated and AI systems on farms in the Philippines: a briefing note for the Bureau of Animal Industry

Lexley Villasis · Briefing note in preparation

Lexley Villasis investigated how farmed animals could be brought into Philippine policy on AI, and she is preparing a briefing note for the Bureau of Animal Industry.
